Checklist

Digital citizenship

Be a Smart & Safe Digital Citizen

Children need practical rules for using technology responsibly.

✅ Good Habits

  • Use a strong, private password and never share it.
  • Ask a trusted adult before entering personal information.
  • Be kind and respectful in messages and games.
  • Check information before believing or sharing it.
  • Take screen breaks and use devices in a healthy way.

🛑 Stop & Ask

  • A stranger asks for your name, address or school.
  • A message makes you feel scared, pressured or uncomfortable.
  • A website asks for a password or payment unexpectedly.
  • A link or download looks suspicious.
  • You see bullying, harmful content or something confusing.
Remember

The 3 Golden Rules

🤫

Keep It Private

Passwords, address, phone number and school stay private.

💬

Be Kind

Type only what you would say politely face to face.

🙋

Tell an Adult

If something feels wrong, stop and tell a trusted adult.
